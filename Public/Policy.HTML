Return & Refund Policy

Last updated: January 2025

We want you to be completely happy with your purchase. If something's not right, we'll make it right — no hassle.

01 Return Window

You may return any item within 7 days of delivery for a full refund or exchange. After 7 days, we are unable to process returns unless the item is defective.

02 Eligible Return Conditions

To qualify for a return, your item must meet the following conditions:

  • Item is unused and in its original condition
  • Original packaging is intact
  • All accessories and components are included
  • Proof of purchase (order number or receipt) is provided

03 Non-Returnable Items

The following items cannot be returned:

  • Items damaged due to misuse or improper handling
  • Items without original packaging
  • Used consumable products (e.g. cleaning solutions)
  • Items marked as "Final Sale" at time of purchase

04 Defective or Wrong Items

If you receive a damaged, defective, or incorrect item, please contact us within 48 hours of delivery. We will arrange a free replacement or full refund — no return shipping cost to you.

Please send us a clear photo of the issue via WhatsApp so we can resolve it quickly.

05 Refund Process

Once your return is received and inspected, we will notify you of approval or rejection. Approved refunds are processed as follows:

  • Cash on Delivery orders: refund via JazzCash / EasyPaisa within 3–5 business days
  • Online Transfer orders: refund to original payment account within 3–5 business days
  • Exchange: replacement item dispatched within 1–2 business days of return receipt

06 Return Shipping

For standard returns (change of mind), the customer is responsible for return shipping costs. For defective or incorrect items, we cover all return shipping costs.

Please ship returns to the address provided by our WhatsApp support team. Do not return items without prior confirmation.

07 How to Initiate a Return

  • Message us on WhatsApp with your order number and reason for return
  • Send photos of the item if it is damaged or incorrect
  • Our team will respond within 24 hours with next steps
  • Pack the item securely and ship to the address we provide

Need help with a return?

Our support team is available 9am – 9pm, 7 days a week.
